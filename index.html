Chapter 1 · The End

Logen flees barefoot through a wet forest after a Shanka attack has scattered him from the Dogman and the rest of his companions. A spear-wielding Shanka catches him among the trees, but Logen uses the terrain to evade it and kills it with his axe. Before he can recover the weapon, a much larger Shanka tackles him and the two tumble downhill toward the gorge beside their camp. Logen catches a root at the cliff edge, only to find the Shanka hanging from his ankle and biting into his leg. Exhausted and unable to climb, he reflects bitterly on his violent life and chooses the river below over the rocks. Remembering his father's practical advice about acting rather than fearing action, he launches himself outward. The Shanka strikes the cliff and dies; Logen hits the river and is dragged beneath the cold water, leaving his survival and his companions' fates unresolved.
