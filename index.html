Chapter 1 · The End

Separated from his companions during a Shanka attack, Logen fights his way through a wet forest while trying to stay alive. He kills one spear-wielding attacker with his axe, but a larger Shanka tackles him and the two tumble toward a gorge. Logen catches a root at the cliff edge, only to find the Shanka hanging from his ankle and biting his leg. As his grip fails, he reflects bitterly on the violence and disappointment of his life and recalls practical advice from his father. With no safe escape, he chooses the river below over the rocks, throws himself away from the cliff, and falls. The Shanka strikes the rock face and dies; Logen hits the water and is dragged into the cold darkness, leaving his survival unresolved.
